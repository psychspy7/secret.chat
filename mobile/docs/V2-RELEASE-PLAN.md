## Goal and preservation contract
A calm, original, responsive messenger with join-first home, accessible controls, small purposeful motion and real loading/empty/offline states. Preserve application ID com.kittycorp.sidechat, original signing certificate, existing Supabase/Firebase project identities, encrypted on-device history/invitations, verified administrator authority, private-room encryption and explicit research consent. Research retention stays manual-clear only. No private message or account data is sent to Kitty Corp/KITTY AI automatically. Keep the independent repositories and their histories; do not edit other app source or company payment/membership work.

Coalesce writes and prevent delayed pending writes from resurrecting cleared drafts or crossing accounts. Proof-first for meaningful bounds/concurrency contracts, focused tests only; no installs/builds/Git writes.
